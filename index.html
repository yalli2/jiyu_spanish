The boot: los verbos e → ie

Tap a verb. Tap any form to hear it. Turn on Hide mode, say the form, then tap to check yourself.

Shaded boxes are inside the boot: yo, tú, él/ella/Ud., ellos/ellas/Uds. change e → ie. Nosotros and vosotros stay outside the boot and keep the e. The endings are the same as a regular verb.

¡Ojo!

If you pronounce the verb form correctly, you will write it correctly. Say it out loud as you write.

¿Lo sabes?

Empezar and comenzar (to begin) need a before an infinitive: Empiezan a jugar. Comienzan a jugar.

¡Así se dice!

Tener follows the same pattern, except the yo form: tengo, tienes, tiene, tenemos, tenéis, tienen.
